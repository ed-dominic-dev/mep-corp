Who is MEP?

MEPCorp is the parent company of the MEP group of businesses. Behind it is Pangilinan, a businessman who has spent decades building companies, with real estate and the leasing of office spaces at the heart of the group.

Today the group reaches into property, technology, power, transport and food, all guided by one idea: build businesses that last and serve their clients well.
